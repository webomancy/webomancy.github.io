A FEW DETAILS

Before we
begin.

Webomancy works with interior designers and architects in Kerala, including studios in Kochi and across the state.

What does Webomancy handle for our studio?

We build and maintain your portfolio website, improve your Google Business Profile, develop locally relevant project pages and run client review requests. Your completed projects become useful website stories and updates that help homeowners understand your work.

Can we keep our existing website?

Yes. We first review your website, local search visibility and enquiry process. The audit identifies what can be improved and whether a rebuild would be useful. The agreed scope depends on what your studio needs.

How do you help us receive better fitting enquiries?

Your portfolio explains your style and approach. Enquiry forms ask about the project, budget and timing before a call. This helps your team assess fit, while local SEO helps relevant homeowners discover the studio.

What does the six month roadmap cover?

We begin with your Google Business Profile, review system and portfolio website. Then we build search visibility around the luxury projects you want, publish completed project stories and track progress through monthly reports.

How does the voice review system work?

Clients follow a link and describe their experience in a voice note. They can edit and approve their draft before publishing their own review. We invite honest feedback without choosing who to ask based on how positive their review might be.
